  1. Notes on Models for Cement Systems

Thermodynamic databases

Cement models for each cement system are available for two thermodynamic databases (CEMDATA and ThermoChimie)


Cemdata18 

The Cemdata18 thermodynamic database (Lothenbach et al., 2019) is specifically developed for hydrated Portland, calcium aluminate, calcium sulfoaluminate, phosphate and blended cements, as well as for alkali-activated materials in the thermodynamic range between 0°C to (at least) 100°C. It requires also the PSI/NAGRA 12/07 database (Thoenen et al., 2014) for aqueous, gases and common minerals (included in the PHREEQC database) and supplemented with the information for zeolites (Ma and Lothenbach 2020a, 2020b, 2021).


Cemdata18 Please add is the version used in the v1.00 of the HPx Module Cementitious Materials.


ThermoChimie

The ThermoChimie database (Madé et al., 2025) is the main output of the ThermoChimie project steered by three partners (three partners: l’Agence nationale pour la gestion des déchets radioactifs (Andra; France), Nuclear Waste Services (NWS; UK) and the Belgian National Agency for Radioactive Waste and Enriched Fissile Material (ONDRAF/NIRAS; Belgium).) Its intended use  is in fields of low-temperature aqueous and geochemical systems with special emphasis on on cement and clay-based systems in support of research for management of radioactive waste.


Jacques (2025) compared simulations of blending and several degradation processes between CEMDATA and ThermoChimie v11a. Several recommendations in that report have led to an update of possible cement phases in ThermoChimie v13.


ThermoChimie V13 is the version used in the v1.00 of the HPx Module Cementitious Materials.
